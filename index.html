Residency projects

I've made three potential prototypes to address frictions surrounding call rescheduling, establishing clear learning objectives, and establishing clear rotation expectations.

All prototypes are using sample data only, but would be reworked to utilize the existing file/data storage formats currently used by the relevant sites.

Demos

01

Call trades

Online tool for residents at a call site to upload their site's call schedule, enter trade request parameters, and find suitable trades automatically.

Learning objectives

Filter through Royal College learning objectives, as related to various rotations. Learners can rate their confidence and flag areas where they hope to receive teaching during said rotation. This plan can be exported and potentially included in a pre-rotation start agreement.

Rotation agreement

Record learner and supervisor expectations before a rotation. Each person can mark a draft as acknowledged, then print or save the agreement as a PDF.
